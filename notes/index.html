The Inner Ledger

Private notes, kept before the story is told.

Before a ride becomes a story, it is only ever contact — road under the wheel, wind past the helmet, the quiet sense of moving through something far larger than you are. The machine goes quiet under you somewhere past the second fuel stop, and what's left standing is just the hills, the light changing, and how small you are against them, in the good way.

Only after that does it become a note: a place name jotted at a fuel stop, an odometer figure, an hour more felt than counted, a thought that hasn't decided what it means yet. Right now, this is the whole toolkit — a phone for stills, a location pin for the map, and whatever the keyboard's dictation mic can catch before the moment thins out. The helmet camera hasn't arrived yet; the vlogging waits with it.

Ride long enough and the mental impressions along with the it's mask disappears. What's left is only the road, the hills, and everything larger than you moving through them with you.
