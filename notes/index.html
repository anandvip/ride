The Inner Ledger

Private notes, kept before the story is told.

Before a ride becomes a story, it is only ever a note. A place name jotted at a fuel stop. An odometer figure, a rough time, a thought that hasn't decided what it means yet. Right now, this is the whole toolkit — a phone for stills, a location pin for the map, and whatever the keyboard's dictation mic can catch before the moment thins out. The helmet camera hasn't arrived yet; the vlogging waits with it.

Private. Local to this device. Written for no reader but the one who rode it.
